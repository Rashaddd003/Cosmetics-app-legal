Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 16, 2026

Your privacy—and the security of your photos—is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information BeautyLook collects, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use BeautyLook, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address (managed securely via Supabase Authentication).
  • Skin Profile: Skin type, concerns, and sensitivity levels.
  • Assessment Photos: Facial photos you upload for AI analysis. These are processed securely and used only for your skin reports.
  • Assessment Data: AI-generated insights regarding acne, texture, hydration, and other skin metrics.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App to help us improve the AI models.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Generate personalized AI skin reports and progress tracking.
  • Recommend skincare products tailored to your skin type.
  • Improve the accuracy of our skincare AI models.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. AI Data Processing & Third-Party Sharing

To provide skin analysis, BeautyLook utilizes secure third-party AI services, including Groq (Llama models) and specialized image processing tools.

What data is sent: We transmit your skin assessment photos and basic profile data (concerns, skin type) to our AI partners for real-time analysis. We do not share your name or email address with these AI providers.

Security: Photos are transmitted via encrypted connections. Our partners are prohibited from using your photos to train their public models or for any purpose outside of your specific assessment.

4. Data Storage & Photo Security

Your data and photos are stored securely using Supabase with high-grade encryption. Facial photos are treated as sensitive biometric data and are protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) so that only you can access them.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data or facial photos to third parties. We share data only:

  • With AI partners solely for processing your skin assessments.
  • With Apple for secure payment processing.
  • When required by law or legal process.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data as long as your account is active to show your skin progress over time. If you delete your account, all associated data and facial photos are permanently purged from our servers.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and export your skin data.
  • Delete your account and all associated photos.
  • Withdraw consent for AI processing.

8. Children's Privacy

BeautyLook is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
